## Robot Image
Upload a robot image to display instead of the default white square. 
More options will open once you have selected an image:
- `Img Scale`: The scale of the image relative to the robot size
- `Img Offsets`: Move the robot off center or rotate it
- `Img Opacity`: Adjust the transparency of the image

## Units & Sizes
Configuration for the displayed route:
- `Units`: Inches, tiles, feet, or centimeters. 
- `Field Rotation`: Rotate the entire field and any routes on it. Visual only
- `Robot Size`: The dimensions of the robot.

## Offsets
Move the displayed route off center or rotate it. Visual only. Used for adjusting a route that is off center or rotated.

## Speed Normalization
What should the min and max speeds of the robot be? By default, values are `0` - `127`.

## Fields
Set what fields should be shown in the selector:
- `Competition`: Shows only fields belonging to that competition
- `Show previous years' fields`: Show fields from previous years

## Analytics
Enable analytics to help us improve MotionView. Captured data includes data such as operating system, app version, and approximate location. Analytics data is associated with a randomly generated identifier and does not include your name, email address, or other directly identifying information.
